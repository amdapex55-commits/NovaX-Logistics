What a parcel to Okara costs

Rs 250 for the first kilogram and Rs 85 for every kilogram after it, whichever NovaX city you ship from. That is the whole price: fuel, handling and cash collection are included, and nothing is added after booking. Each started kilogram counts, so a 1.2 kg parcel is charged as 2 kg.

How a parcel reaches Okara

Okara and Adda Gamber opened as NovaX delivery stations on 17 October 2026. Parcels are booked and collected in your city as usual, then handed to the NovaX rider who covers Okara, Adda Gamber and the Sahiwal towns for the last mile, with the same tracking and the same COD reconciliation as every other NovaX parcel.

  1. You book. Pick Okara or Adda Gamber under Destination City, enter the customer's name, phone, full address and the COD amount, or upload a whole sheet of orders. Shopify, WooCommerce and the Merchant API book these towns too.
  2. We collect from you. A rider picks the parcel up free from your shop or warehouse in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad or Rawalpindi, between 11 am and 9 pm on working days.
  3. It travels to Okara. Every change of status is recorded with the time it happened, and your dashboard updates as it does.
  4. It is delivered and the cash collected. Your customer gets a tracking link they can open without an account.

Parcels to Okara are delivered in 2–3 working days.

Addresses riders can find

Give the mohalla, colony or chak number, the street, a landmark such as a mosque, school or market, and a phone number that will be answered. Write the city as Okara or Adda Gamber even when the address names a road to another city, such as Lahore Road. A clear address and a reachable phone decide whether a parcel is delivered on the first attempt.

When a customer refuses

A refusal or an unreachable customer shows up on your dashboard straight away, with the rider's note. You decide what happens next: ask for another attempt, or have the parcel returned to you. A returned parcel is charged its delivery fee, and no COD is collected, so it is worth confirming large or unusual orders with the customer by phone before you book.

How your cash comes back

The rider collects the COD amount at the door. It is reconciled against that parcel's AWB, the delivery charge is deducted on the same line, and the balance is credited to your NovaX wallet. Every credit, charge and payout is a row in your ledger, so you can see exactly which parcel each rupee came from, then withdraw at the speed you choose.

Questions sellers ask about Okara

How much does a COD parcel to Okara or Adda Gamber cost?

Rs 250 for the first kilogram and Rs 85 for each additional kilogram, all inclusive, from Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad or Rawalpindi. A 2 kg parcel is Rs 335 and a 3 kg parcel is Rs 420. There is no fuel surcharge and no separate cash-collection fee.

How long does delivery to Okara or Adda Gamber take?

2–3 working days from collection. You can follow every step on your dashboard, and your customer can follow it through their own tracking link.

Can NovaX collect parcels from me in Okara or Adda Gamber?

Not yet. NovaX delivers to Okara or Adda Gamber, and collects free from sellers in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

How do I book a parcel to Okara or Adda Gamber?

Choose Okara or Adda Gamber under Destination City on the New booking form. Bulk upload, the Shopify app, WooCommerce and the Merchant API accept it too.

What happens if my customer refuses the parcel?

It appears on your dashboard with the rider's note, and you choose whether to reattempt or return it. A returned parcel is charged its delivery fee and collects no COD.

Do I need a contract or a minimum number of parcels?

No. There is no contract, no monthly fee and no minimum. You pay only for parcels you actually ship, and your workspace opens as soon as you sign up.

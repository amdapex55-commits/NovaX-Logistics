FAQ

Questions sellers ask before booking.

Still unsure? Ask Autopilot inside the portal — it answers from your own account.

What does “AI-run courier” actually mean?

Humans do the physical work — riders collect and deliver. Everything technical is automated: order import, AWB generation, rider allocation by zone and cash limit, customer notifications and exception handling.

How fast is delivery, really?

Karachi to Karachi is same-day when booked before cutoff, and next-day otherwise. Karachi to Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi is 2–3 working days. Pickup itself averages about two hours from request.

What happens right after I sign up?

Your workspace is created immediately — no review queue, no waiting for approval. The moment signup completes you’re taken straight into your seller portal with the booking desk ready.

Can I connect Shopify or WooCommerce?

Yes — both, in a few minutes. New orders flow in as parcels ready to book with no re-entry, and delivery status syncs back to your store. Every webhook is HMAC signature-verified and idempotent.

Can my team have separate logins?

Yes, with real scoped permissions. Warehouse staff get booking and label printing only. Finance gets invoices and reports. Support gets tracking and customer replies. Only the owner can request money.

How does same-day COD settlement work?

When a rider marks a parcel delivered and the cash is reconciled, the net payable is credited to your wallet immediately — not queued for a weekly batch. You then withdraw at the speed you choose.

What does it cost to try?

Nothing upfront. There’s no setup fee, no monthly charge and no contract — you only pay per parcel you actually ship, and setup takes about five minutes.

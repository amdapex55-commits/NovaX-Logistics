Common questions

Everything sellers ask before booking.

Open any question for the same core information presented on the live NovaX homepage.

What does “AI-run courier” mean?

Riders still collect and deliver. NovaX automates order import, AWB generation, customer updates, exception detection, reporting and settlement around that physical journey.

How fast is delivery?

Karachi deliveries target same or next day. Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi generally take 2–3 working days after dispatch.

What happens after signup?

Your seller workspace opens immediately so you can create a booking and generate an AWB in the same session.

Can I connect Shopify or WooCommerce?

Yes. Store orders can enter NovaX without re-entry and delivery status can sync back to the original order.

Can my team have separate access?

Team roles can separate booking, finance and support access while keeping owner-only actions protected.

How does COD settlement work?

Once delivery and cash are reconciled, the net amount becomes available in your wallet. The payout fee is shown before confirmation.

What does it cost to try?

There is no setup fee, monthly charge or long-term contract. Delivery is charged per parcel.

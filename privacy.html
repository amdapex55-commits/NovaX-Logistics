Privacy Policy

NovaX Logistics is a cash-on-delivery courier in Pakistan. To move a parcel from a seller to a buyer we need to know who the parcel is for, where it is going and how to reach that person on the doorstep. This page says exactly what we hold, why, for how long, and how to get it back or have it deleted.

Last updated 26 September 2026 · NovaX Logistics, Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan

Who this covers

Two different people appear in our records, and they have different relationships with us:

  • Merchants — the sellers who book parcels, through the NovaX portal, our API, or the NovaX app for Shopify. We are the data controller for a merchant's own account information.
  • Consignees — the people receiving a parcel. We hold a consignee's details because the merchant asked us to deliver to them. For that data the merchant is the controller and NovaX is the processor: we use it to deliver the parcel and for nothing else.

What we collect about a consignee, and why

Four fields, and only these four, are needed to deliver a parcel. We ask Shopify for no other customer data.

FieldWhy a courier cannot work without it
NameThe rider has to hand the parcel to the right person and record who signed for it. It is also what a consignee is asked for when they call to ask where their parcel is.
Shipping addressThe physical destination. It also decides the route, the delivery zone and therefore the price.
Phone numberRiders in Pakistan call before arriving; addresses here are frequently descriptive rather than exact. Without a phone number the first attempt fails and the parcel returns to the seller.
Order reference and cash-on-delivery amountThe amount to collect at the door, and the reference the merchant uses to reconcile it.

We do not collect or store a consignee's email address, payment card, date of birth, government ID, or the contents of the order beyond a category the merchant chooses for handling.

Merchants using the NovaX app for Shopify

When a merchant installs our Shopify app, Shopify sends us each new order so it can be booked as a parcel automatically. The app requests the minimum access needed for that:

  • Order data, from which we read the shipping name, address and phone and the cash-on-delivery amount. Everything else in the order payload is ignored.
  • Fulfillment access, so that when a rider delivers the parcel we can mark the Shopify order fulfilled and write the tracking number back. This is a write of our own delivery outcome, not a read of the merchant's customers.

The raw Shopify order payload is held only until the parcel is booked and reconciled, and is cleared on request through Shopify's own privacy webhooks — see Deletion below. Uninstalling the app stops all further data flow immediately.

What we collect about a merchant

  • Business name, contact name, phone number and email address, to open and operate the account.
  • Pickup addresses and cities.
  • Wallet and settlement records — parcels booked, cash collected, fees charged, payouts made. These are financial records.
  • Sign-in and audit records: who changed what, and when.

Who else sees this data

  • NovaX riders and operations staff, limited to the parcels they are handling.
  • Supabase, our database and application host. Data is stored in the Asia-Pacific (Sydney) region.
  • Cloudflare, which serves novaxlogistics.com and routes application traffic.
  • Shopify, for merchants who connect a Shopify store, in the direction described above.

We do not sell personal data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use consignee data to market to consignees.

How long we keep it

  • Raw Shopify order payloads — cleared once the parcel is delivered and reconciled, or immediately on a redaction request.
  • Parcel delivery records — retained as commercial and financial records. A parcel is evidence that cash was collected and owed to a merchant, so the record of the delivery is kept even after a redaction request; the Shopify payload attached to it is not.
  • Merchant account and wallet records — retained for as long as the account exists, and afterwards for as long as Pakistani tax and accounting rules require.
  • Access and audit logs — kept on a rolling window, currently 30 days.

Access, correction and deletion

If you are a consignee

Ask the seller you bought from. They are the controller of your details, and they can ask us on your behalf — or you can contact us directly using the details below and we will answer within 30 days.

If you are a merchant

Your account data is visible and editable in the NovaX portal. To export or close an account, contact us.

Deletion

We honour Shopify's mandatory privacy requests automatically. A customers/data_request is recorded and answered by our operations team within 30 days. A customers/redact clears the stored Shopify payload for the orders named in the request. A shop/redact, which Shopify sends 48 hours after an app is uninstalled, clears the stored payloads for that shop and destroys our access token for it.

Security

  • Every request is authenticated, and the database enforces per-account row-level access rather than relying on the application to filter.
  • Traffic is HTTPS only.
  • Webhook deliveries are rejected unless they carry a valid signature.
  • Access to protected customer data is logged at the point it is read.

Children

NovaX is a business service. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, for merchants, notify the account contact.

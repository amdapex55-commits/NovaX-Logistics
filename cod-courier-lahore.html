What a parcel to Lahore costs

Rs 225 for the first kilogram and Rs 85 for every kilogram after it. That is the whole price: fuel, handling and cash collection are included, and nothing is added after booking. Each started kilogram counts, so a 1.2 kg parcel is charged as 2 kg.

Karachi to Lahore is our busiest route outside Karachi

Most NovaX sellers are Karachi brands, and after Karachi itself, Lahore is where more of their customers live than anywhere else. The route is set up for that volume: parcels are collected in Karachi, sorted at the hub, and moved to Lahore for delivery.

  1. You book. Enter the customer's name, phone, full Lahore address and the COD amount, or import a whole sheet of orders at once. The AWB is created on the spot.
  2. We collect in Karachi. A rider picks the parcel up from your address and it is scanned in at the hub.
  3. It travels to Lahore. Every change of status is recorded with the time it happened, and your dashboard updates as it does.
  4. It is delivered and the cash collected. Your customer gets a tracking link they can open without an account.

When a customer in Lahore refuses

A refusal or an unreachable customer shows up on your dashboard straight away, with the rider's note. You decide what happens next: ask for another attempt, or have the parcel returned to you. A returned parcel is charged its delivery fee, and no COD is collected, so it is worth confirming large or unusual orders with the customer by phone before you book.

How your cash comes back

The rider collects the COD amount at the door. It is reconciled against that parcel's AWB, the delivery charge is deducted on the same line, and the balance is credited to your NovaX wallet. Every credit, charge and payout is a row in your ledger, so you can see exactly which parcel each rupee came from, then withdraw at the speed you choose.

Questions sellers ask about Lahore

How much does it cost to send a COD parcel from Karachi to Lahore?

Rs 225 for the first kilogram and Rs 85 for each additional kilogram, all inclusive. A 2 kg parcel is Rs 310 and a 3 kg parcel is Rs 395. There is no fuel surcharge and no separate cash-collection fee.

How long does Karachi to Lahore take?

2–3 working days from collection in Karachi. You can follow every step on your dashboard, and your customer can follow it through their own tracking link.

Do you collect from sellers based in Lahore?

Collection runs from Karachi. If your stock ships from Lahore, talk to our sales team before you book, so we can confirm collection for your address.

What happens if my Lahore customer refuses the parcel?

It appears on your dashboard with the rider's note, and you choose whether to reattempt or return it. A returned parcel is charged its delivery fee and collects no COD.

Can my customer in Lahore track the parcel?

Yes. Every parcel has a tracking link your customer can open on their phone without signing up, showing where the parcel is and when it was last updated.

Do I need a contract or a minimum number of parcels?

No. There is no contract, no monthly fee and no minimum. You pay only for parcels you actually ship, and your workspace opens as soon as you sign up.

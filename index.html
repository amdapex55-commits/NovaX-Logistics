09 / FAQ

Questions sellers ask before booking.

What does "AI-run courier" actually mean?

Humans do the physical work — riders collect and deliver. Everything technical is automated: order import, AWB generation, rider allocation by zone and cash limit, customer notifications, exception detection with aging timers, invoice generation and wallet settlement. A seller shipping 100 parcels a day never manually touches that chain. Autopilot AI sits on top, reading your live account data to answer questions and take actions on request.

How fast is delivery, really?

Intra-city (Karachi to Karachi) is same-day when booked before cutoff. Inter-city between Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi is next-day. Pickup itself averages about two hours from booking. Every parcel carries an SLA timer, and if it breaches, the exception engine escalates it automatically rather than waiting for you to notice.

What happens right after I sign up?

Your workspace is created immediately — no review queue, no waiting for approval. The moment signup completes you're taken straight into your seller portal with the booking desk ready, so you can create your first AWB in the same session.

Can I connect Shopify or WooCommerce?

Yes — both, in a few minutes. New orders flow in as parcels ready to book with no re-entry, and delivery status syncs back to your store. Every webhook is HMAC signature-verified and idempotent, so nothing untrusted can create an order on your account and a platform retry can never duplicate a parcel.

Can my team have separate logins?

Yes, with real scoped permissions. Warehouse staff get booking and label printing only. Finance gets invoices and reports. Support gets tracking and customer replies. Only the owner can request payouts. Roles are enforced on the server, not just hidden in the interface.

How does same-day COD settlement work?

When a rider marks a parcel delivered and the cash is reconciled, the net payable is credited to your wallet immediately — not queued for a weekly batch. You then withdraw at the speed you choose: instant at 0.7%, 12 hours at 0.3%, or 24 hours at 0.1%. The fee is shown before you confirm and itemised on the ledger afterwards.

What does it cost to try?

Nothing upfront. There's no setup fee, no monthly charge and no contract — you only pay per parcel you actually ship, and setup takes about five minutes. If it doesn't work for your brand you leave, and you keep every record.

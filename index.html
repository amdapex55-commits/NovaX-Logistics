    How fast is delivery?

    Karachi to Karachi is same-day when booked before cutoff, and next-day otherwise. Every other route, to or from Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi, is 2–3 working days. Riders collect from your door between 11 am and 9 pm on working days.

    When do I get my COD money?

    When a rider marks a parcel delivered and the cash is reconciled, the net amount is credited to your wallet immediately — not queued for a weekly batch. You then withdraw at the speed you choose: instant (usually within 2–3 hours) at 0.7%, 12 hours at 0.3%, or 24 hours at 0.1%. The fee is shown before you confirm and itemised on your statement.

    What does it cost?

    Rs 225 to a Karachi address and Rs 250 to Lahore, Islamabad or Rawalpindi, plus Rs 85 for each extra kg. There's no setup fee, no monthly charge and no contract — you only pay for the parcels you actually ship.

    What if my customer refuses the parcel?

    The rider records it and you see it on your dashboard straight away, so you can ask for a re-attempt. If it comes back, it is returned to you and the normal delivery charge applies — there is no extra return fee. No COD is collected, so nothing is credited for that parcel.

    Can I connect my Shopify store or website?

    Yes. Install the NovaX app from the API tab in your portal and connect it with a one-time code: new Shopify orders are booked with an AWB automatically, and Shopify shows them fulfilled with tracking once the rider collects. Any other website can book through the NovaX Merchant API, and any store can upload a day's orders from a spreadsheet.

    Can my team have separate logins?

    Yes, with real scoped permissions. Warehouse staff get booking and label printing only. Finance gets invoices and reports. Support gets tracking and customer replies. Only the owner can request payouts. Roles are enforced on the server, not just hidden in the interface.

    What happens right after I sign up?

    Your workspace is created immediately — no review queue, no waiting for approval. You go straight into your seller portal with the booking desk ready, so you can create your first AWB in the same session. We ask for a photo of the front and back of your CNIC, which only NovaX staff can see, so we know who owns the account and who we pay.

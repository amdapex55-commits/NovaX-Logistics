Support

NovaX Logistics is a cash-on-delivery courier in Pakistan. If a parcel, a booking, a payout or the NovaX app for Shopify is not doing what it should, this is how to reach a person.

Support hours: 9am to 8pm Pakistan Standard Time (UTC+5), Monday to Saturday

Contact

In the portal — merchants can open a ticket from the Support tab, and replies appear in the same place.

From Shopify — the NovaX app has a Problem? button on every order. It opens a ticket already linked to that order and its AWB, so you do not have to explain which parcel you mean.

We answer WhatsApp within an hour during support hours, and every ticket within one working day.

What we can help with

  • A parcel — where it is, a failed delivery attempt, a reattempt, a return, or a recall after pickup.
  • A booking — an order that did not become a parcel, a wrong address, a wrong COD amount, or a cancellation.
  • The Shopify app — connecting your store, orders not arriving, tracking not appearing in Shopify, or booking rules not behaving as you expect.
  • Money — COD you are owed, an invoice, a deduction you do not recognise, or a payout that has not arrived.
  • Your account — sub-accounts for your team, pickup addresses, and closing an account.

Connecting the Shopify app

Install the NovaX app from Shopify. It will ask for a connect code. Open the NovaX portal, go to Integrations, copy the code shown there, and paste it into the app. The code proves you own the NovaX account — we never attach a store to an account by matching an email address.

The code lasts 20 minutes and works once. If it expires, press New code.

Orders that arrive before you connect are held, not lost. They are booked the moment the connection is made.

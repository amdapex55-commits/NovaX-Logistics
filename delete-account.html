Delete your NovaX account

This page is for NovaX Logistics merchants: the sellers who book parcels with us in the NovaX portal or app. It explains how to delete your account and the data that goes with it.

Updated 28 November 2026

Delete it yourself, in the app or on the web

  1. Sign in to NovaX: open the app, or go to novaxlogistics.com/client.html.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Scroll to Delete your NovaX account and press Ask to delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Only the account owner can do this. A team member's login does not show the option.

Can't sign in?

Email support@novaxlogistics.com from the email address on the account, with the subject Delete my NovaX account. Or message us on WhatsApp at 0312 3922558. We confirm it is you before we delete anything.

What happens next

  • Within 30 days we close the account and email you when it is done. You can cancel from Profile until then.
  • Parcels already on the road are delivered or returned first. Your wallet balance is paid out to your bank account.
  • Deleted: your login, your profile and business details, your CNIC photos, your team logins, your saved pickup addresses and your store connections (Shopify, WooCommerce, API keys).
  • Kept: booking, delivery and payment records that Pakistani tax and commercial law requires us to keep. They are kept for as long as Pakistani tax and accounting rules require, without your login, and are not used for marketing.

If you are a buyer, not a merchant

You do not have a NovaX account. To have your delivery details deleted, see our privacy policy.

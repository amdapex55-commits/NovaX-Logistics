NovaX vs TCS: which COD courier is better for online sellers?

For online sellers whose orders go to NovaX's 12 cities, NovaX is usually the better deal: flat published rates, no COD fee or percentage, and COD in your wallet the day the parcel is delivered. TCS's strength is the biggest courier network in Pakistan, with nationwide and international reach and a name every customer knows, so it is the better choice when you ship to towns outside NovaX's 12 cities, or send parcels abroad.

Where NovaX wins

  • You keep more of every order. NovaX takes no COD fee and no percentage. The only deduction is the flat delivery charge you saw when booking.
  • Your cash does not wait for a cycle. COD is credited to your wallet the day the parcel is delivered, and Nova Saver withdrawals to your bank are free.
  • Refusals cost less. A refused parcel comes back at the normal delivery charge with no extra return fee. Nova Recover can phone the customer and sell it again. The rider's voice note shows exactly what happened at the door.
  • One price list, in public. Rs 225 to a Karachi address, Rs 250 to the other stations, Rs 85 per extra kg. No slabs to negotiate and no volume minimum.
  • Built for online stores. Shopify and WooCommerce orders book themselves, the Merchant API takes bulk orders, and the portal works on a phone.

Questions sellers ask

Is NovaX cheaper than TCS?

NovaX charges a flat Rs 225 to a Karachi address and Rs 250 to its other stations for the first kg, plus Rs 85 per extra kg, with no COD fee and no percentage. TCS sets prices per account, so compare its written quote, including its COD fee, against these numbers.

How fast does NovaX pay COD compared with TCS?

NovaX credits COD to your wallet the day the parcel is delivered. Withdrawals to your bank are free on Nova Saver (48–72 hours), Rs 100 on Nova Express (within 24 hours) or Rs 500 on Nova Bolt (6–12 hours). TCS pays on its own schedule, which you should confirm with them.

Does NovaX deliver everywhere TCS does?

No. NovaX delivers to 12 cities: Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Sahiwal, Okara, Chichawatni, Harappa, Yousuf Wala and Adda Gamber. TCS covers more of Pakistan, so many sellers use NovaX for these cities and another courier for the rest.

Can I use NovaX and TCS together?

Yes. There is no minimum volume and no contract lock-in, so you can send your orders for NovaX's cities through NovaX and the rest through your current courier.

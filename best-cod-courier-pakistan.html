Five questions to ask any courier before you sign

  1. What is the COD fee, in rupees, on a Rs 3,000 order? A percentage grows with every sale. NovaX's answer is zero.
  2. On which day does the cash reach my bank? Get the remittance cycle in writing. NovaX credits your wallet the day the parcel is delivered.
  3. What do I pay when a customer refuses? Some couriers add a return fee on top of delivery. NovaX charges the normal delivery charge only.
  4. Is there a minimum volume or a contract? NovaX has neither.

Do couriers in Pakistan charge a COD fee?

Most nationwide couriers charge a COD handling fee, often tiered by order value or a percentage with a minimum. NovaX charges no COD fee.

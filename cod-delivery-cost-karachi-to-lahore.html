How much is COD delivery from Karachi to Lahore?

Rs 250 for the first kilogram and Rs 85 for each extra kilogram, all inclusive. A Karachi to Lahore parcel is delivered in 2–3 working days, and pickup from your shop is free.

Updated 29 September 2026

What you pay, by weight

Parcel weightKarachi → Lahore
Up to 1 kgRs 250
Up to 2 kgRs 335
Up to 3 kgRs 420
Up to 5 kgRs 590

Each started kilogram after the first adds Rs 85, so 1.4 kg is charged as 2 kg.

Conditions

  • The price follows where the parcel is delivered: Rs 250 to a Lahore, Islamabad or Rawalpindi address, Rs 225 to a Karachi address — whichever of the four cities we collect from.
  • No GST, no COD withholding tax, no fuel surcharge and no separate cash-collection fee.
  • Pickup is free from your shop or warehouse in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad or Rawalpindi, between 11 am and 9 pm.
  • If the customer refuses and the parcel comes back, the delivery charge still applies; there is no extra return fee.

Example

A 1.2 kg dress to a customer in Lahore with Rs 3,500 COD: the delivery charge is Rs 335 (Rs 250 + Rs 85). The rider collects Rs 3,500 at the door, and Rs 3,165 is yours after the charge.

What the flat rate actually means

Most Karachi couriers quote a base rate and then add weight slabs, fuel surcharges, a COD-handling percentage and sometimes a distance charge. By the time the cash reaches you the number no longer resembles the quote.

NovaX charges Rs 200 per parcel up to 1 kg, anywhere inside Karachi. There is no per-kilometre component and no separate COD fee. Distance-based pricing was removed from the platform entirely — a parcel from Saddar to Gulshan-e-Iqbal and a parcel from Clifton to North Karachi cost the same.

Your cash, the day it lands

When a rider marks a parcel delivered and the cash is reconciled, the net payable is credited to your NovaX wallet immediately — not queued for a weekly batch. Every credit, delivery charge and payout is written to an append-only ledger, and each row references the AWB it came from, so your balance is the sum of its rows rather than a figure you have to trust.

Questions Karachi sellers ask

How much does a COD courier cost in Karachi?

Rs 200 flat for any parcel up to 1 kg anywhere inside Karachi. There is no per-kilometre charge, no fuel surcharge and no separate COD-handling fee, so the rate you see when you book is the rate deducted from your cash-on-delivery amount.

Is same-day delivery available across Karachi?

Yes, for intra-city parcels booked before the daily cutoff. A parcel booked in Gulshan-e-Iqbal for delivery in DHA is a same-day job; the rider is allocated by zone as soon as the AWB is created.

When do I actually receive my COD cash?

The day the parcel is delivered. Once the rider marks it delivered and the cash is reconciled, the net payable is credited to your NovaX wallet immediately rather than held for a weekly or fortnightly batch. You then choose the payout speed.

Which areas of Karachi do you cover?

All 143 areas listed on this page, covering DHA and Clifton through to Korangi, Landhi, Orangi, North Karachi, Malir and Bin Qasim. If your area is on the list it is serviceable today, and the booking form validates it before you pay.

Do I need a contract or a minimum volume?

No. There is no contract, no monthly fee, no setup fee and no minimum number of parcels. You pay per parcel you actually ship, and your workspace opens immediately at signup with no approval queue.

Can I connect my Shopify or WooCommerce store?

Yes. Orders arrive as parcels ready to book with no re-entry, and the delivery status syncs back to your store automatically. Every webhook is signature-verified.

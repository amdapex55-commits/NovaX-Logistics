## Full description (Play 4000 max / Apple description)
NovaX is the courier for online stores in Pakistan. Book cash-on-delivery parcels, print labels, follow every delivery live and get your COD paid out, all from your phone.

FOR MERCHANTS
• Book a parcel in seconds: customer, city, COD amount and weight. The price shows before you book.
• Print or share the label (A4 or thermal), one parcel or a whole batch.
• Free pickup in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi: ask for a pickup and a NovaX rider comes to your door.
• Follow every parcel live: picked up, in transit, out for delivery, delivered, refused or returned.
• Notifications when a parcel is picked up, delivered or refused, and when a payout is sent.
• COD wallet: the cash your customers pay comes into your NovaX wallet. Withdraw to your bank with flat fees, never a percentage.
• Connect your Shopify or WooCommerce store and book orders without typing them again.
• Reports, invoices and a full history of every rupee.
• Help in the app: open a ticket on any parcel, or message us on WhatsApp.

FOR CUSTOMERS
• Track any NovaX parcel with its tracking number. No account needed.

FOR NOVAX RIDERS
• Riders sign in with the login NovaX gives them to collect, deliver and record each parcel.

Rates are on novaxlogistics.com. A merchant account is free to open. Payouts go only to the bank account you add, and the account owner's CNIC is checked to keep your money safe.

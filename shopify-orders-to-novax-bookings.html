How do Shopify orders become NovaX bookings?

Install the NovaX app from the API tab in your NovaX portal and connect your store with a one-time code. From then on, new Shopify orders to Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad or Rawalpindi are booked as NovaX parcels with an AWB automatically — no retyping.

Updated 29 September 2026

Setting it up

  • Open your NovaX portal → API tab → install the NovaX app on your Shopify store.
  • In the app, paste the connect code shown on the API tab. The code proves the store belongs to your NovaX account; it works once and expires after 20 minutes.

What happens to each order

  • Orders with a delivery address in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad or Rawalpindi become NovaX bookings with an AWB, the customer's details and the COD amount.
  • Orders to other cities are skipped, and the app shows why.
  • Print the label from the app or your portal, and request a pickup as usual.
  • When a rider collects the parcel, the order is marked fulfilled in Shopify with the NovaX AWB as its tracking number.

Example

A customer in Rawalpindi places a Rs 2,800 cash-on-delivery order at 9 pm. Within seconds it appears in your NovaX account with an AWB; you print the label next morning, the rider collects it, and Shopify shows it fulfilled with tracking.

Nova Instant rules and privacy

The short rules for sending one parcel by bike inside Karachi. Version 1.0, 5 October 2026.

The service

  • A NovaX bike rider collects one parcel from the pickup pin and takes it to the delivery pin, inside Karachi only.
  • Riders work from 10 am to 1 am.
  • The fare is Rs 25 for each kilometre of road between the two pins, with a minimum of Rs 100. The fare shown before you book is the fare.
  • The fare is paid in cash to the rider: by the sender at pickup, or by the receiver at delivery, as chosen at booking. The rider does not hand a parcel over, or take one, without the cash.

What you can send

  • The parcel has to fit safely on a motorbike and weigh 10 kg or less.
  • It must be worth Rs 10,000 or less.
  • Not allowed: cash, jewellery, gold, prize bonds or cheques; anything illegal; weapons; alcohol or drugs; fuel, gas cylinders, fireworks or anything that can burn or explode; live animals; liquids that are not sealed.
  • Pack the parcel so it survives a bike ride. A rider may refuse a parcel that is not allowed, is not packed, or is not what the booking says.

Your first booking

The first time a number books, NovaX phones the sender to confirm before a rider is sent. If the call is not answered the booking is cancelled.

The delivery PIN

Each booking has a 4-digit PIN on its tracking page. The receiver gives the PIN to the rider to take the parcel. Share the tracking link only with the receiver: anyone with the PIN can collect the parcel.

Cancelling

  • The person who booked can cancel free of charge, from the tracking page, until the rider has collected the parcel.
  • Once the rider has the parcel it cannot be cancelled. It is delivered, or it comes back to the sender.

If the delivery cannot be made

  • If the receiver cannot be reached, refuses the parcel, will not pay the fare, or the address cannot be found, NovaX calls the sender and the receiver.
  • If it still cannot be delivered, the rider brings the parcel back to the pickup address and hands it to the sender against the same PIN.
  • If the receiver was to pay and did not, the sender pays the fare when the parcel comes back.
  • There is no extra charge for the return trip at present.

Loss or damage

Tell NovaX within 24 hours of the delivery or return. NovaX is responsible for a parcel that is lost or damaged while its rider holds it, up to the parcel's proven value and never more than Rs 10,000. NovaX is not responsible for parcels that were not allowed, were badly packed, or were described wrongly.

Your information

  • NovaX keeps what you enter to make the delivery: both names, both mobile numbers, both addresses, the map pins and what the parcel is.
  • The rider sees the two mobile numbers only after accepting the job.
  • Anyone with the tracking link sees the names, the addresses and the PIN, never the mobile numbers. Seven days after a booking is closed, the link shows only its result and dates.
  • To stop fake bookings NovaX also records the internet address and a random code for the browser that made a booking. They are used for that and nothing else.
  • Place search and the map use OpenStreetMap services (Photon by Komoot) and Mapbox: what you type in the search box is sent to them to find the place, and the two pins are sent to a route service to measure the road distance.
  • This phone keeps your recent places, your own name and number, and your bookings, so you can find them again. Help, then Clear saved details, removes them from this phone.
  • NovaX does not sell this information or use it for advertising. Booking records are kept for accounts and disputes.

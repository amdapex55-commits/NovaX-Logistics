Nova Instant rules and privacy

The short rules for sending one parcel by bike inside Karachi. Version 1.2, 6 October 2026.

The service

  • A Nova Instant rider collects one parcel from the pickup pin and takes it to the delivery pin, inside Karachi only. The rider is either a freelance bike rider working with NovaX or a rider on NovaX's own staff. NovaX checks every rider's CNIC before they take a job, and the rules on this page are the same whichever rider comes.
  • Sometimes two riders share one delivery: the first hands the parcel to the second on the way. The tracking page names the rider who has it.
  • Riders work from 10 am to 1 am.
  • The fare is Rs 25 for each kilometre of road between the two pins, with a minimum of Rs 100. The fare shown before you book is the fare.
  • The fare is paid in cash to the rider: by the sender at pickup, or by the receiver at delivery, as chosen at booking. With cash on delivery, the fare is taken out of the cash the receiver pays (see below). The rider does not hand a parcel over, or take one, without the cash.

What you can send

  • The parcel has to fit safely on a motorbike and weigh 10 kg or less.
  • It must be worth Rs 10,000 or less.
  • Not allowed: cash, jewellery, gold, prize bonds or cheques; anything illegal; weapons; alcohol or drugs; fuel, gas cylinders, fireworks or anything that can burn or explode; live animals; liquids that are not sealed.
  • Pack the parcel so it survives a bike ride. A rider may refuse a parcel that is not allowed, is not packed, or is not what the booking says.

Your first booking

The first time a number books, NovaX phones the sender to confirm before a rider is sent. If the call is not answered the booking is cancelled.

The delivery PIN

Each booking has a 4-digit PIN on its tracking page. The receiver gives the PIN to the rider to take the parcel. Share the tracking link only with the receiver: anyone with the PIN can collect the parcel.

Cancelling

  • The person who booked can cancel free of charge, from the tracking page, until the rider has collected the parcel.
  • Once the rider has the parcel it cannot be cancelled. It is delivered, or it comes back to the sender.

If the delivery cannot be made

  • If the receiver cannot be reached, refuses the parcel, will not pay the fare, or the address cannot be found, NovaX calls the sender and the receiver.
  • If it still cannot be delivered, the rider brings the parcel back to the pickup address and hands it to the sender against the same PIN.
  • If the receiver was to pay and did not, the sender pays the fare when the parcel comes back.
  • There is no extra charge for the return trip at present.

Cash on delivery and your wallet

  • Cash on delivery needs a free Nova Instant account. It is separate from NovaX merchant accounts.
  • You set the amount when you book: at least the fare, and at most Rs 10,000 per parcel. The receiver pays the whole amount to the rider in cash at the door. No cash, no parcel.
  • The fare comes out of the cash on delivery. The rest goes to your Nova Instant wallet, first as "on its way" while the rider holds the cash, then as "ready to withdraw" once the rider has paid it in to NovaX.
  • If the receiver refuses the parcel or does not pay, no cash on delivery is collected, the parcel comes back, and the sender pays the fare when it does. If the receiver pays less than the amount, only what was collected reaches your wallet, and NovaX tells you.
  • If a rider has not paid in cash on delivery they collected, NovaX follows it up with the rider; your wallet shows it as on its way until then.
  • You can withdraw what is ready to JazzCash, Easypaisa or a bank account, at least Rs 500 at a time and one request at a time. Your CNIC number is needed before your first withdrawal. NovaX sends each withdrawal by hand and records the transaction ID in your wallet.
  • Every amount in or out of your wallet is listed in its history. A mistake is corrected with a new line that says why; nothing is changed or removed.

Loss or damage

Tell NovaX within 24 hours of the delivery or return. NovaX is responsible for a parcel that is lost or damaged while its rider holds it, up to the parcel's proven value and never more than Rs 10,000. NovaX is not responsible for parcels that were not allowed, were badly packed, or were described wrongly.

Your information

  • NovaX keeps what you enter to make the delivery: both names, both mobile numbers, both addresses, the map pins and what the parcel is.
  • The rider sees the two mobile numbers only after accepting the job.
  • Anyone with the tracking link sees the names, the addresses and the PIN, never the sender's or the receiver's mobile number. Seven days after a booking is closed, the link shows only its result and dates.
  • From the moment a rider accepts the job until it is delivered, returned or cancelled, the tracking page also shows that rider's first name, bike number and mobile number, so the sender or receiver can call them. They are removed from the page when the job closes.
  • To stop fake bookings NovaX also records the internet address and a random code for the browser that made a booking. They are used for that and nothing else.
  • Place search and the map use OpenStreetMap services (Photon by Komoot) and Mapbox: what you type in the search box is sent to them to find the place, and the two pins are sent to a route service to measure the road distance.
  • This phone keeps your recent places, your own name and number, and your bookings, so you can find them again. Help, then Clear saved details, removes them from this phone.
  • A Nova Instant account also keeps your name, mobile, email and usual pickup address; your CNIC number once you withdraw; the account you withdraw to; and your wallet history. Only you and the NovaX office see them.
  • NovaX does not sell this information or use it for advertising. Booking records and wallet history are kept for accounts and disputes.

For Nova Instant riders

  • Riders are freelance. A rider keeps the fares they collect in cash and owes NovaX a commission of 20% of each fare for every finished job.
  • Cash on delivery belongs to the client, not the rider. A rider pays in what they owe (commission and cash on delivery) by JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank or at the NovaX office, and records it in the rider app; NovaX confirms it.
  • A rider holding more than Rs 15,000 owed to NovaX cannot take more cash-on-delivery jobs until they pay in.
  • When NovaX owes a rider (for example, fares taken out of cash on delivery), the rider can ask for a payout of at least Rs 500.
  • Before the first job, a rider sends a photo of their CNIC and a home bill, and an emergency contact. Only the NovaX office sees them.

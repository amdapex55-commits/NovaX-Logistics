What a parcel to Islamabad costs

Rs 225 for the first kilogram and Rs 85 for every kilogram after it. That is the whole price: fuel, handling and cash collection are included, and nothing is added after booking. Each started kilogram counts, so a 1.2 kg parcel is charged as 2 kg.

Getting an Islamabad address right the first time

Islamabad is laid out in sectors, and a sector-style address is the difference between a parcel delivered on the first attempt and one that comes back as "address not found". When you book, include:

  1. The sector and subsector, written the way residents write it, such as F-7/2 or G-11/3.
  2. Street and house number, plus the building and flat number for apartments.
  3. A phone number the customer will actually answer, because the rider calls before arriving.

If the order is going to Rawalpindi rather than Islamabad, book it under Rawalpindi. Both cities are served at the same rate, but the city on the booking decides where the parcel is routed. Karachi to Islamabad is delivered in 2–3 working days.

When a customer in Islamabad refuses

A refusal or an unreachable customer shows up on your dashboard straight away, with the rider's note. You decide what happens next: ask for another attempt, or have the parcel returned to you. A returned parcel is charged its delivery fee, and no COD is collected, so it is worth confirming large or unusual orders with the customer by phone before you book.

How your cash comes back

The rider collects the COD amount at the door. It is reconciled against that parcel's AWB, the delivery charge is deducted on the same line, and the balance is credited to your NovaX wallet. Every credit, charge and payout is a row in your ledger, so you can see exactly which parcel each rupee came from, then withdraw at the speed you choose.

Questions sellers ask about Islamabad

How much does a COD parcel from Karachi to Islamabad cost?

Rs 225 for the first kilogram and Rs 85 for each additional kilogram, all inclusive. A 2 kg parcel is Rs 310 and a 3 kg parcel is Rs 395.

How long does Karachi to Islamabad take?

2–3 working days from collection in Karachi, with every step visible on your dashboard and on your customer's tracking link.

Do you deliver in both Islamabad and Rawalpindi?

Yes, both, at the same rate. Book each order under the city it is actually going to, because that decides how the parcel is routed.

How should I write an Islamabad address?

Sector and subsector first (for example F-7/2), then street and house number, and a phone number the customer will answer. Clear sector addresses are delivered on the first attempt far more often.

What if my customer in Islamabad refuses the parcel?

You see it on your dashboard with the rider's note and decide whether to reattempt or return. A returned parcel is charged its delivery fee and collects no COD.

Is there a contract or minimum volume?

No contract, no monthly fee and no minimum. You pay only for the parcels you ship.

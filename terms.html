Part A · Merchant Agreement

1The parties

  1. This agreement is between NovaX Logistics, of Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan ("NovaX", "we", "us"), and the business or person who opens a NovaX merchant account ("you", the "merchant").
  2. If you open the account for a business, you confirm you are allowed to bind that business. The person named on the account's CNIC record is responsible for the account together with the business.

2Accepting these terms

  1. You accept this agreement by ticking the acceptance box when you sign up, or by booking a parcel, whichever comes first. Part B (the Shipping & COD Policy), our rate card and our Privacy Policy form part of this agreement.
  2. Records kept in NovaX's systems (bookings, status history, rider scans and GPS points, wallet ledger, invoices and messages) are accepted as evidence of what happened, unless you show they are wrong.

3The service

  1. NovaX collects parcels from you, delivers them to your customers in the cities listed in B1, collects cash on delivery ("COD") where you ask us to, and pays the COD to you through your NovaX Wallet after our charges.
  2. We act as your carrier and collection agent. We are not the seller of your goods and are not a party to the sale between you and your customer.
  3. Delivery times we publish (for example, same or next day in Karachi and 2–3 working days to other cities) are targets. Late delivery does not entitle you to compensation or a refund of charges, except as set out in B7 for lost parcels.
  4. Integrations (Shopify, WooCommerce, our Merchant API) are provided as they are. We are not responsible for failures, outages or changes on platforms we do not run, or for orders your store does not send to us.

4Your account

  1. You must give true, complete details, including the owner's CNIC, and keep them up to date. We may limit bookings or payouts until identity checks are complete.
  2. You are responsible for everything done through your account, including by team members (sub-accounts) and through your API keys or store connections. Keep passwords and keys private. We are not liable for loss caused by someone using your credentials.
  3. Payouts are made only to a bank account or wallet in the name of the account holder or the registered business. You are responsible for the bank details you enter. We are not liable for a payout sent to details you gave us.
  4. Sales representatives and referral partners are not NovaX employees and cannot change, waive or add to these terms.

6COD and your NovaX Wallet

  1. Our responsibility for COD starts when the rider records the cash as collected. It does not cover money a customer did not pay, paid to someone else, or paid outside the NovaX process.
  2. COD is credited to your wallet after the rider hands the cash in and we reconcile it against the parcel. The timetable in B9 describes how this normally works.
  3. Payout times shown on our website or in marketing are targets. Bank holidays, bank or wallet provider delays, reconciliation checks and the holds in section 7 can make payouts later, and a later payout does not entitle you to interest or compensation.
  4. You can withdraw your available balance to your verified bank account or wallet at any time, subject to section 7.
  5. Invoices show the COD collected, our charges and the amount credited to you. Tell us about any mistake within 15 days of the invoice; after that the invoice is treated as accepted.
  6. If we pay you an amount by mistake, you must return it, and we may recover it from later COD or your wallet.
  7. Inactive accounts. An account with no bookings for 60 consecutive days is inactive. If COD is still pending on an inactive account, we may take up to 30 days after the last pending parcel is delivered or returned to reconcile and pay it.
  8. When an account closes, we pay the final balance within 30 days after every open parcel has been delivered or returned and all charges are settled.

  4. We may ask merchants with high return rates or repeated disputes to prepay charges or keep a minimum wallet balance before booking more parcels.

Part B · Shipping & COD Policy

B1Where we deliver

Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Bookings to other cities are refused at booking. If a parcel reaches us addressed outside these cities, it is marked "Out of service area" and returned to you at the return charge.

B2Prohibited items

Do not send: cash, cheques, prize bonds or other money; gold, silver and other precious-metal or precious-stone jewellery (artificial and fashion jewellery is allowed); precious stones; weapons, ammunition and replicas; drugs, narcotics, alcohol and prescription-only medicine; explosives, fireworks, gas cylinders, aerosols in bulk, flammable liquids and other dangerous goods; live animals and plants; human or animal remains; perishable food that needs refrigeration; counterfeit or pirated goods; anything illegal to sell or carry in Pakistan. We may refuse, hold, open for inspection, return or hand to the authorities any parcel we reasonably believe contains a prohibited item, and the charges and any costs remain payable.

B3Packing

Pack every parcel to survive normal handling, stacking and transport by motorbike and van: a sealed outer box or tamper-evident flyer, cushioning for anything breakable, leak-proof inner packing for liquids, and the NovaX label fixed flat on the outside. A parcel we judge unsafe to carry may be refused at pickup.

B4Weight

We weigh parcels on our scale at processing. That weight sets the charge, rounded up to the next kilogram after the first. If you disagree with a weight, raise a ticket with a photo of the parcel on a scale within 48 hours of the change; after that the measured weight is final.

B5Delivery attempts

Each parcel gets one delivery attempt and one reattempt. If the customer refuses, is unavailable, or cannot be reached at the phone number given, the rider records the reason. After the reattempt, or straight away if the customer refuses, the parcel is returned to you. You may ask for the reattempt once per parcel through the portal. Riders do not let customers open parcels before paying unless you choose "Allow to open" at booking, and we are not responsible for disputes that follow an opened parcel.

B6Returns

A refused or returned parcel costs the normal delivery charge, which is deducted from your COD. You must accept returned parcels at your pickup address. If a return cannot be handed back within 7 days because you are unavailable or refuse it, we may charge storage at the rate in the portal, and after 30 days' notice we may dispose of it without liability. Check returned parcels when you receive them and report a problem within 48 hours.

B7Lost parcels

  1. Report a parcel you believe is lost through a support ticket within 15 days of the booking date.
  2. We investigate for up to 30 days. A parcel not found in that time is treated as lost.
  3. For a lost parcel we pay the lowest of: the COD amount (or, for a prepaid parcel, the value you declared at booking); the cost price you can show with a purchase invoice; or Rs 5,000. We also refund the delivery charge for that parcel. Payment is made to your NovaX Wallet.
  4. A parcel later found is delivered or returned in the normal way, and any compensation paid for it is recovered from your wallet.

B8Damage

We do not compensate for damage, breakage, leakage, spoilage or missing contents. If a parcel's outer packing arrives visibly torn or opened, the customer or you may refuse it and the rider will record photos where possible; we will review the case, but any goodwill credit is at our discretion and is not an admission of liability.

B9How payouts normally work

  1. The rider collects COD and records it at delivery.
  2. The rider hands the cash in to NovaX, and we reconcile it against the parcel.
  3. An invoice is generated for your delivered and returned parcels: COD collected, minus our charges.
  4. The net amount is credited to your NovaX Wallet, and you can withdraw it to your verified account.

Steps 2 to 4 usually happen on the day the parcel is delivered. Section 6 and the holds in section 7 still apply.

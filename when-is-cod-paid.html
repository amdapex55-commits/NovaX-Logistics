When is COD paid?

When a rider marks a parcel delivered and the cash is reconciled, the net payable is credited to your NovaX wallet immediately — not queued for a weekly batch. You then withdraw at the speed you choose: Nova Saver (48–72 hours) is free, Nova Express (within 24 hours) is a flat Rs 100, and Nova Bolt (6–12 hours) is a flat Rs 500.

Each fee is a flat amount for one withdrawal. It does not grow with the amount you withdraw.

Conditions

  • The amount credited is the COD collected minus NovaX's delivery charges for those parcels.
  • The withdrawal fee is shown before you confirm and itemised on your wallet ledger afterwards.
  • Shipping itself has no GST, no COD withholding tax and no hidden charges; the withdrawal fee is the only charge on your money, and Nova Saver has none.

Example

Three parcels deliver with Rs 12,000 COD and Rs 700 in delivery charges: Rs 11,300 is credited to your wallet. Withdrawing all of it on Nova Saver costs nothing; on Nova Express you receive Rs 11,200.

When is COD paid?

When a rider marks a parcel delivered and the cash is reconciled, the net payable is credited to your NovaX wallet immediately — not queued for a weekly batch. You then withdraw at the speed you choose: Express (within 12 hours) at 0.7%, Standard (12–24 hours) at 0.3%, or Saver (24–48 hours) at 0.1%.

Conditions

  • The amount credited is the COD collected minus NovaX's delivery charges for those parcels.
  • The withdrawal fee is shown before you confirm and itemised on your wallet ledger afterwards.
  • Shipping itself has no GST, no COD withholding tax and no hidden charges; the withdrawal fee is the only charge on your money.

Example

Three parcels deliver with Rs 12,000 COD and Rs 700 in delivery charges: Rs 11,300 is credited to your wallet. Withdrawing all of it on Saver costs Rs 11.30.

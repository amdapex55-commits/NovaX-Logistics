Your money

A promise this specific has to be built, not claimed.

Fifteen minutes only works if the accounting underneath is exact. Here is what actually enforces it.

01

An append-only ledger

Every credit, fee and payout is a permanent row. Nothing is edited after the fact, so your balance can always be rebuilt from its own history.

02

A mismatch blocks the payout

If the ledger and your balance ever disagree, NovaX stops the payment and shows the gap instead of paying out a number it cannot prove. Silence is the failure mode we designed against.

03

Labels never leave our systems

Barcodes and QR codes are generated inside NovaX. Your tracking numbers and customer details are never handed to a third-party image service to draw a label.

Questions

The things sellers actually ask.

Is fifteen minutes real, or an average?

It is a ceiling on the instant tier, not a median. You request a withdrawal and the transfer leaves NovaX within fifteen minutes. Your bank's own posting time is outside our control, which is why we state the part we control.

What does it cost to send a parcel?

Karachi is priced by road distance from your pickup point: Rs 120 covers the first six kilometres, then Rs 20 per kilometre, capped at Rs 320. Inter-city is a flat Rs 180 for the first kilogram plus Rs 60 per additional kilogram. The calculator above is the same rate card the portal charges.

What happens if a customer refuses the parcel?

The rider photographs the doorstep and records the refusal, and the parcel is routed back to you. You are charged the delivery fee for the trip out and back — netted off your COD on the same invoice, never billed separately.

Do you integrate with my store?

Shopify and WooCommerce orders import automatically. There is also a generic webhook if you run something custom, and a CSV importer that validates every row — city, phone, COD, weight, duplicate order IDs — before a single AWB is generated.

Which areas do you actually cover?

Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Karachi is broken into more than 150 named areas so pricing reflects the real distance rather than a city-wide flat rate. If your area is not listed at booking, tell us and we will map it.

How do I know my COD balance is right?

Your wallet shows an append-only ledger — every credit, fee and payout with its source and timestamp. If the ledger ever disagrees with your balance, the system blocks the payout and flags it rather than quietly paying a number it cannot prove.
